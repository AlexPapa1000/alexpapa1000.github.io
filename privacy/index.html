Toy Physics: Catch Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Summary

Toy Physics is free and supported by advertising. We do not collect any personal information ourselves. Our advertising provider, Google AdMob, does collect information in order to serve and measure ads. Your game progress stays on your device.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We operate no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. We never see your data.

What is stored on your device

Saved locally so the game works between sessions, and never transmitted:

  • which levels you have unlocked, per difficulty
  • stars earned on each level
  • music and sound-effect volume settings
  • your advertising consent choice, where one was requested

Deleting the app removes all of it.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. To do this, Google receives:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be inferred
  • device and software information — model, operating system version, language
  • ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped

Google acts as an independent controller of this information. What it does with it is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services — policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • About AdMob advertising — support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

This happens whether or not you allow personalised advertising. Withholding permission means you see non-personalised ads, which still require the data above to be served and counted; it does not stop advertising altogether.

Your choices

On iOS, the first time ads load, the system asks whether Toy Physics may track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining means Google is not given your IDFA for personalised advertising. You can change this later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. Deleting it stops personalised advertising.

In the EEA and the UK, you are asked to consent before personalised ads are shown, through a consent form provided by Google. You can change that choice at any time from the app's Options screen.

Anywhere, you can review Google's own ad settings at adssettings.google.com.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience. If you believe a child has used the app and you have questions about advertising, contact us at the address below. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we instruct our advertising provider to apply the restrictions appropriate to the audience we declare to the app stores.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. For data held by Google as a result of advertising, use Google's own controls linked above. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, delete the app.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
