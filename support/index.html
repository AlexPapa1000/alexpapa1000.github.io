Common questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Unlocked levels and stars are stored locally, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?
In the EEA and the UK, open the app's Options screen and tap Privacy Settings. On iOS you can also change tracking permission in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.
